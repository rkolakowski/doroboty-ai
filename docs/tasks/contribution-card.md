# Candidate contribution card

Evaluate how a role-specific answer changes recruiters' interpretation of individual contribution and invitation choices. Reuse the application form, CV snapshot, employer record and existing stage actions.

- The role's configured question is snapshotted with the submitted answer. Offers without a configured question retain their existing form.
- In the employer workspace, `zestaw=a|b` and `material=cv|karta` select one of four comparison views for six prepared fictional applicants. Both variants show the same CV for a given profile. The card variant adds the candidate's answer beneath the CV. Scores and summaries are hidden in both variants.
- The research facilitator guide lives in the project vault outside the repository. It holds the scoring key and session sequence.

## Employer-configured questions

The offer editor accepts an optional screening description and one list of up to ten questions. Employers choose a text answer or single choice with two to ten distinct options. Drafts reuse answers by question identity, and submitted applications retain their original questions, options and answers. Offers without configured lists keep the single-response form. Review sets A and B use their prepared snapshots.
